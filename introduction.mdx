Scanmex Logistics Tracker is a two-application system that replaces paper-based delivery workflows with real-time GPS tracking. Before the project, drivers left the warehouse carrying printed address sheets — if a street name was wrong, a customer moved, or traffic forced a detour, the office had no way to know until a driver called in. The solution connects a web dashboard for the logistics team with an Android app for drivers on Zebra rugged terminals, both sharing a single SQL Server database and synchronized with SAP Business One through its OData v4 Service Layer.

## The problem

<Note>
  Scanmex faced two root problems: the company had **no visibility into where its fleet was at any moment**, and it could **not give customers any ETA** for their incoming deliveries.
</Note>

Drivers departed the warehouse each morning with nothing but a printed sheet of delivery addresses. Any change — a wrong address, a last-minute re-route, unexpected traffic — stayed invisible to the office team until a driver picked up the phone. The result was a steady stream of inbound calls asking "where is my order?", with no reliable data to answer them and no way to make informed dispatch decisions.

## Project context

Scanmex Logistics Tracker was developed as a professional residency (*residencia profesional*) by Alonso Alberto Cortés Morales at Instituto Tecnológico de Álvaro Obregón (ITAO). The project was carried out within a real company environment, giving the system genuine operational requirements: an existing SAP Business One installation, an active fleet of Zebra rugged terminals, and a logistics team that needed a practical tool rather than a prototype. For a detailed walkthrough of the before-and-after workflow and the architectural decisions made along the way, see the [case study](/case-study).
